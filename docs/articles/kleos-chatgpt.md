# Kleos + ChatGPT: your marketing team, inside the chat you already use

Kleos is an AI marketing team for small businesses. It learns your business from your website, plans a month of posts,
articles and newsletters in your voice, publishes what you approve to the channels you connected, and measures what
happened, including whether AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend you. Connected to ChatGPT, all of that is one question
away.

## Connect it

1. In ChatGPT, open **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and switch on **Developer mode**.
2. In **Apps & Connectors**, choose **Create**. Name it **Kleos**, paste `https://app.getkleos.ai/mcp` and choose
   **OAuth**.
3. Kleos opens: sign in with your usual login, pick your workspace and choose **Allow**. You can allow reading only.
4. In a chat, add Kleos from the tools menu.

No API key, on every Kleos plan. You need a Kleos workspace: [start one free at getkleos.ai/start](https://getkleos.ai/start).

## Ask it

**"What's on my Kleos calendar this week?"**
Every planned post, article and newsletter, with the channel, day, time and where it stands: planned, waiting for
review, approved, scheduled or published.

**"Write a post about the biggest problem my buyers have."**
ChatGPT reads your buyers and their problems from Kleos and asks Kleos to write the post. Kleos's own writer and checks
do the writing, in your business's voice, and the draft waits in your review queue.

**"Post this exactly as I wrote it, on LinkedIn tomorrow at 9."**
Your words go in word for word, with no AI rewriting. Kleos's checks come back as notes; you decide.

**"What's waiting for my approval? Approve the ones that are ready."**
ChatGPT lists the queue and approves only the drafts Kleos marks ready. A draft its checks flagged can't be approved
until it's fixed, and ChatGPT tells you what the check found.

**"How did last month go? Did our posts bring any leads?"**
Posts published, impressions, clicks and leads over the last 30 days.

**"Does ChatGPT recommend my business when people ask my buyers' questions?"**
Kleos checks the questions your buyers ask in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity each week, and shows where you're
named, where a competitor is named instead, and which questions nobody answers with you yet.

## You stay in charge

- A draft is a draft: nothing is published because you asked for writing.
- Publishing needs someone with publishing permission in your workspace, or a business with auto-publish switched on.
- Every request uses your own role and your plan's allowances, the same as clicking in Kleos.
- Disconnect at any time under **Settings → Integrations → Connected assistants** in Kleos.

More, with troubleshooting: [getkleos.ai/chatgpt](https://getkleos.ai/chatgpt).
